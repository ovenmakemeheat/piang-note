# About Me

![Portrait of Guntee Doungmanee](../../assets/guntee-profile.jpg)

I’m Guntee Doungmanee, a computer engineering student at [King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT)](https://www.kmutt.ac.th/en/) in Bangkok. I’ve worked in machine learning research, data science, and AI engineering and tinkering around those.

I’m interested in computer vision, generative and multimodal models, and scientific imaging—especially super-resolution, learning from limited data, and model generalization.

## Experience

- **[Present] AI Engineer (part-time), [@QuanXAI](https://quanxai.com/)**. -- Building a financial AI agent with Bedrock AgentCore, LangGraph, and PostgreSQL-backed tools.
- **Data Scientist Intern, [@AXONS](https://axonstech.com/)** -- Developed poultry-weight forecasting and casual inference across 45 farms and 1,000+ houses.
- **AI Engineer Intern, [@Bangkok Silicon](https://bks.net/)** -- As Presales Team, I Built prototypes for tree-carbon estimation and wild-elephant image retrieval.
- **Student Research Assistant, [@AI Singapore](https://aisingapore.org/)** -- Contributed Thai and Southeast Asian language data to [Project SEALD](https://aisingapore.org/aiproducts/southeast-asian-languages-in-one-network-data-seald/)’s multilingual LLM safety evaluation.
- **NLP Research Contributor, [@NECTEC LST](https://www.nectec.or.th/en/)** -- Worked on Thai dialect sentiment analysis, named-entity recognition, and semantic search for Thai herbs.
