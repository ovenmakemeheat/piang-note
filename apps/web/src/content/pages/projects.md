# Projects

My projects include an assistant for outpatient visits, a video-captioning system, Thai vector fonts, and image search for wild elephants.

## Research and engineering

- **MALI: Ambient Clinical Agent** — An assistant for outpatient consultations that transcribes speech and extracts clinical facts. It was recognized at the [Thailand ICT Awards 2026](https://www.portcas.com/competition/thailand-ict-awards-2026).
- **[GemmaClip](https://buildpodium.com/projects/gemmaclip-7444a5)** — An evidence-grounded video-captioning system built with Gemma 4, ROCm, and vLLM. It placed first in the Google DeepMind track at [AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT II](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-developer-hackathon-act-ii), among 189 submissions.
- **[High-Quality Thai Vector Fonts](https://github.com/ovenmakemeheat/ThaiVecFont)** — Adapted DeepVecFont-V2 to Thai glyphs represented as Bézier curves, reaching a 0.049 L1 reconstruction error across a 1,000-font dataset. The project received the Outstanding AI Project Award in [AI Builders](https://ai-builders.github.io/) Program in 2024.

## Health and multisensory design

- **[OITEP](https://www.linkedin.com/company/144011218/)** — Co-founded a startup since 2024, exploring whether scent could make lower-salt food taste saltier and we already have nursing homes partnership; the project placed first at [First Siriraj x MIT Hacking Medicine (2024)](https://hackingmedicine.mit.edu/post/siriraj-x-mit-hacking-medicine-2024), placed second at [Siriraj x MIT Hacking Medicine Beyond Hack (2026)](https://sirirajxmithackmed.com/) and received a [TED Youth startup grant](https://tedfund.mhesi.go.th/).
