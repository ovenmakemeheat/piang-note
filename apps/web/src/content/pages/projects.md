# Projects

My projects include an assistant for outpatient visits, a video-captioning system, Thai vector fonts, and image search for wild elephants.

## Research and engineering

- **MALI: Ambient Clinical Agent** (Jan 2026–present) — An assistant for outpatient consultations that transcribes speech and extracts clinical facts. It was recognized at the Thailand ICT Awards 2026.
- **GemmaClip** (Aug 2026) — An evidence-grounded video-captioning system built with Gemma 4, ROCm, and vLLM. It placed first in the Google DeepMind track at AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT II, among 189 submissions.
- **High-Quality Thai Vector Fonts** (May–Jul 2024) — Adapted DeepVecFont-V2 to Thai glyphs represented as Bézier curves, reaching a 0.049 L1 reconstruction error across a 1,000-font dataset. The project received the Outstanding AI Project Award.
- **Tree Carbon Estimation and Elephant Re-identification** (Jan–Mar 2026) — Built a tree-carbon estimation prototype with an MAE of 14.37 and an image-retrieval system for matching wild elephants across photographs.

## Health and multisensory design

- **OITEP** (Nov 2024) — Co-founded a project exploring whether scent could make lower-salt food taste saltier. We ran a 20-person pilot; the project placed first at MIT Hacking Medicine and received a TED Youth startup grant.
